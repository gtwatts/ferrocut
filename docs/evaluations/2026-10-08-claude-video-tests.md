### Patterns

- **Strong:** exact spec, loudness on target, checker pass and clean journals in all five; cuts or entrances on beat grids; captions placed from measured speech; coherent, eased design systems; 4–9 look-and-revise rounds per producer; audio-only fixes re-rendered no video.
- **Weak:** nobody listened, and the measured mixes show it (pumping ducks, a sub-heavy trailer score, digital silence in bar 1, a buried bed). Single-frame artifacts fall between sampled stills (caption blinks from 33 ms cue gaps). Four of five open on an empty or near-black frame. Some brief features are ticked rather than used (push-ins too small to see, a badge "added to tick the nested-comp box"). Three producers altered pictures to pass checker heuristics.
- **Tool friction:** media stretched to the output frame before the transform (four producers, about 83 minutes); no text metrics (four); checker cut, black and loudness defaults (five); transcription errors (four); AAC true-peak overshoot (four); no per-bus loudness readout (three); absolute font paths from `captions import` (two).

### Did the reports hold up?

On numbers, yes: every headline figure in all five reports reproduced (duration, frames, rate, loudness, true peak, checker result, journal), apart from a 0.1 LU loudness-range difference and stale paperwork. Craft claims fared worse: trailer's "clean 12 fps cadence" (3-1-2 in the render) and reframes that "read as new shots" (not t4); vertical's "her turn-away" (bare wall in its own cut sheet); vox's "portable relative paths" (27 absolute font paths), "25 tracks" (24) and missed-cut warnings dismissed as fades (20 bracket real caption dropouts); kinetic's promised bar-1 "sub pulse" (digital silence). Every "what is weak" section was candid and all five admitted never listening, but the explainer's cast credits and the vox caption blinks went unreported.
