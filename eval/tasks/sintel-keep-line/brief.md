# Keep one line of dialogue

`timeline.json` holds a 24-second dialogue scene from *Sintel* (`media/d1.mkv`, picture and sound) on track `V1`.

Cut the scene down to **just the shaman's line "So, what brings you to the land of the gatekeepers?"**:

- one clip, starting at 0 on the timeline;
- the whole line must be audible, with a short handle (no more than about half a second of extra sound before
  the first word and after the last);
- none of the other lines ("Thank you." just before it, "I'm searching for someone." after it) may be heard.

Render the result to `out.mkv`. The scene has camera cuts of its own; those are expected in the render.
