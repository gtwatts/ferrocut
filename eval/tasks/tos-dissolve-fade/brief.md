# Dissolve and fades

`timeline.json` has two *Tears of Steel* shots on track `V1` (picture only) with a hard cut at 5 s. Each clip
in `media/` is 6 s long, so there are handles before and after the parts in use.

1. Replace the hard cut with a **1-second cross-dissolve centred on the cut**, running from 4.5 s to 5.5 s.
2. **Fade in from black** over the first second, and **fade out to black** over the last second.

The sequence stays 10 s long. Render the result to `out.mkv`.
